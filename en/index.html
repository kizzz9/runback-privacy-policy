Privacy Policy · for Apple Music

Your sets stay with you —
on your iPhone and in your iCloud.

Runback doesn’t gather your information on the developer’s servers. There are no ads and no tracking. This page explains what the app handles and where.

Nothing collectedNo account. No usage data is sent to the developer.
No trackingNo advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
Only yoursSets are stored on this iPhone and in your own iCloud.

01Overview

Runback (the “App”) is an iPhone app provided by an independent developer (the “Developer”). The App has no mechanism for the Developer to collect information that identifies you or how you use it. It includes no third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

02Information stored on this iPhone

The following stays inside the App’s storage on this device (including its App Group) and is never sent to the Developer.

SetsName, emoji, song order (Apple Music song IDs, titles, artist names, artwork URLs, genres), tags, cover settings
Cover imagesAn image you picked from Photos or made with Image Playground
Play recordsWhen and how often you played sets in the App (used for recent sets and “your usual at this hour”)
Estimated listening timesTime-of-day records estimated from Music’s Recently Played (can be turned off or cleared in Settings)
Smart mixesRules, starting points, and the song IDs used last time
OtherDrafts in progress, display settings, the list and small images for the widget, playback diagnostics (recent state changes)

03iCloud sync

Sets (name, song order, tags, cover, play records) are stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple’s CloudKit and sync between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Only you can access that database; the Developer can’t see its contents. To stop syncing, turn off Runback in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Other information in the table above, such as estimated listening times and drafts, doesn’t sync.

04Apple Music

With your permission, the App uses Apple’s MusicKit to search and play Apple Music, read your library and playlists, and add songs you choose to your library, favorites, and playlists. This communication is with Apple and is handled under Apple’s privacy policy. You can revoke permission at any time in the Settings app.

05Network connections

The App connects only to the following Apple services — never to the Developer’s servers.

  • Apple Music (MusicKit / Apple Music API): search, playback, library actions, charts, genre list
  • iTunes Search API (itunes.apple.com): when saving from the share sheet, to look up a song’s title and details from the shared link’s song ID
  • Apple’s artwork delivery: images for covers and the widget
  • iCloud (CloudKit): syncing sets (your own private database)

06Share sheet

When you choose the App from Share in Music or elsewhere, it identifies the song from the shared link and saves it to the set you pick, on your device. Links and song details are not sent to the Developer.

07Photos and image generation

If you use a photo as a cover, only the one image you pick in the picker is read. The App doesn’t ask for access to your whole photo library. Image Playground generation is performed by Apple.

08Sharing a set

Share Set sends a file containing the set’s name, emoji, song list, tags, and cover settings to the people or apps you choose. Photo covers and play history aren’t included. You choose the destination; it doesn’t pass through the Developer.

09Backup files

Files exported with Backup & Restore are saved where you choose (such as the Files app). You control where they are stored and shared; the Developer never receives them.

10Contact form

What you send through the support page’s form (name, email, and message; some fields optional) is used only to reply. It passes through Cloudflare Workers and is forwarded to the Developer’s email by Resend. It is not shared with third parties or used for any other purpose.

11Sharing with third parties

Because the Developer doesn’t collect your information, it is not shared with or sold to third parties, except where required by law.

12Children

The App is suitable for all ages and doesn’t collect information from children.

13Deleting data

You can delete sets and tags in the App, and clear history in Settings → History & Privacy. Deleting the App removes what it stored on this device. Sets synced to iCloud remain and come back if you reinstall. To delete them from iCloud too, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete Runback’s data (exported backup files remain).

14Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with the revision date.

15Contact

For questions about this policy, use the contact form on the support page.
